The following things can appear as loot and have rarities:
1. Techs
	1. I don't currently foresee any situation where the party doesn't have all four jobs, but if it did, only techs usable by someone in the party can drop
2. Gear
3. Items

4. Loot has 5 categories
	1. Low Level Common
	2. Low Level Rare
	3. High Level Common
	4. High Level Rare
	5. Legendary
	6. High Level and Legendary loot can only appear as rewards for boss fights, or once the party reaches level 4 (After the settlement defense)

5. There are 4 Loot Buckets:
	1. Level 1-3 Fights
		1. 85% Low Level Common
		2. 15% Low Level Rare
	2. Level 1-3 Bosses & Chests
		1. 95% Low Level Rare
		2. 5% High Level Rare
	3. Level 4-5 Fights
		1. 20% Low Level Rare
		2. 65% High Level Common
		3. 15% High Level Rare
	4. Level 4-5 Bosses
		1. 70% High Level Rare
		2. 30% Legendary
	5. Level 4-5 Chests
		1. 90% High Level Rare
		2. 10% Legendary

6. Items, Gear, and Techs shouldn't be in one pool

7. How Loot is Obtained:
	1. Normal fights drop 1/2/3 (60%/25%/15%) pieces of loot off the "Fights" bucket of their level
	2. Elite fights drop 3-4 (50%/50%), also off the "Fights" bucket
	3. Chests drop 1
	4. Bosses drop 3

8. Restrictions:
	1. A Single Reward:
		1. Cannot drop the same loot more than once
		2. Cannot drop two techs for the same job
		3. Can drop at most one legendary
		4. Cannot drop a legendary that the player has already taken (even if the player doesn't currently have it). If the legendary pool is empty, drop a High Rare instead
			1. Draft choices not selected can still appear later
		5. If it drops one tech, it is less likely to drop a second (how is this enforced?)
		6. Cannot drop a tech already known to someone in the party
			1. It can drop gear and items already held
		7. Cannot have more than one draft (see Drafting in Act 2+)
	2. Maybe some kind of system where the longer you go without getting a rare, the higher the chances get?

9. Drafting in Act 2+:
	1. Starting in Act 2, every piece of loot has a 20% chance to spawn as a draft (because Nym forgot what was supposed to go there)
		1. This % chance increases in Act 3 to 30%
	2. The player is offered 3 options, all selected from the same type (gear, item, tech) and rarity
	3. Draft options abide by all restrictions on loot (Can't show the same thing twice, can't repeat legendaries)
	4. Techs and rarer rewards should be more likely to draft, somehow

10. The Doubt Gauge, perks, boons & curses can modify:
	1. The % drop chance of individual buckets
	2. The number of items dropped by buckets
	3. The % chance of drafts
	4. The effects of drafts
11. Cedric's Spirit Meter as a Rogue gives draft rerolls
