After the player enters Act 3, they unlock The Doubt Gauge. (As Nym's doubt grows, the power of The Demon King waxes stronger...)

The Doubt of a run is a numerical measure of how difficult it is.  Runs with no doubt modifiers are considered Doubt 0

When starting a new run in Act 3, the player is presented with a random assortment of run modifiers, each of which has an integer value associated with it.  Positive numbers make the run harder, negative numbers make the run easier.

Sample Doubt Modifiers (These will all be varieties of [triggered](triggered-effects) or [passive](passive-effects) effects):
1. Slippery - Doubt +1: All enemies gain 10 [evasion](evasion) at the start of each round
2. Truce - Doubt +1: All entities gain P Dwn 1 at the start of the battle
3. Slim Pickings - Doubt +1/+3: When drafting, you are offered 1/2 fewer options to choose from
4. Plentiful - Doubt -2: All fights drop an addition loot

You earn additional [specializations](character-specializations) for your characters by clearing runs with successively higher doubt values.  You unlock the next tier by clearing a run with doubt above the your current doubt level, and doing so raises your doubt level by 1.  If you clear with higher than the required doubt, you still only unlock 1 tier and the threshold is still raised by only 1.
