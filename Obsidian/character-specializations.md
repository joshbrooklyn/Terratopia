As you clear runs with higher and higher values of [doubt](doubt-gauge), you gain the ability to improve each character's innate power.

All [characters](characters) have the first tier of specializations unlocked at the beginning of Act 3.  Clearing a run with higher doubt than your current doubt threshold unlocks the next tier of specializations.

Each character has their own set of 6 tiers of specialization. Each tier has 2 options, which can be toggled between freely outside of a run.  Once a run has been started with a particular set of specializations, they cannot be changed until the run is over. Generally the lower tiers are simpler, and more "Number go up", and the higher tiers get more complicated

1. Akio
	1. Tier 1
		1. Max Hp +20%
		2. Defense +10%
	2. Tier 2
		1. "Good"/"Perfect" increases the dmg of a tech by an additional 10/15%
		2. Successful timed hit attacks (using the fight command) have their pwr increased by an additional 15%
	3. Tier 3
		1. The "Good" window for QTEs is 50% larger. Doesn't affect the size of "Perfect"
		2. *Something that enhances Timed hit attacks or rewards precision and intentionality*
	4. Tier 4
		1. You may re-attempt a failed QTE once per turn+. Second try QTEs cannot be perfect
		2. The QTE "Perfect" window is 100% larger.  Removes the "Good" timing window (making the only possible results "Perfect" and "Miss")
	5. Tier 5
		1. Clearly identifies the perfect window
			1. *Will this work for all QTEs?*
		2. Each successive Perfect QTE increases the bonus of perfects by 10% (up to +50%). Resets at battle end or on first "Miss" result, "Good" result, or failed fight cmd. Doesn't reset when taking a turn that doesn't have a timing element.
	6. Tier 6
		1. When you miss a QTE or timed hit, the window expands. If you land a "Good" QTE, the "Perfect" window increases
		2. Every time you succeed at a QTE or timed hit, the window reduces in size and increases in effectiveness. "Good" doesn't reduce the size of "Perfect". Resets on "Miss".
