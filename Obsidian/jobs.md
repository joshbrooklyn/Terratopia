1. Your job determines:
	1. What perks you get access to on level up
	2. What techs you can equip from loot sources
		1. Some perks and techs are available to all multiple jobs
	3. Your job ability
2. There are 4 jobs:
	1. Fighter
		1. Ability - Cover - Pick an ally (can include self) All actions directed at that ally are redirected to you. Take 25% less damage from all sources.  This effect lasts until the beginning of your next turn.
			1. Some actions unredirectable (eg most healing or buff effects)
	2. Rogue
		1. Ability - Evade - Gain 100% [Evasion](evasion)
	3. White Mage
		1. Ability - Pray - Restore 15% of all allies missing Hp. Cannot restore Hp above 50% max
	4. Black Mage
		1. Ability - Charge - Restore 15% of your missing Tp. Cannot restore Tp above 50% max
3. The game is balanced around always having a party with one of each job
	1. Maybe some wacky settings can change that later
